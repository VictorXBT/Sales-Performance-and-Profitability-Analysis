# Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales performance and profitability using the AdventureWorks dataset. The analysis explores revenue, product costs, gross profit, gross profit margin, product performance, and sales territory performance.

The goal is to identify key business trends, understand what drives profitability, and provide insights that can support better business decisions.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Power BI Desktop:** Data modelling, analysis, DAX calculations, and dashboard development.
- **Power Query:** Data cleaning and transformation.
- **DAX:** Creating measures and calculating key performance indicators (KPIs).
- **AdventureWorks Dataset:** Source of the sales, product, customer, reseller, date, and territory data.

## Business Questions

The analysis was guided by the following questions:

1. How much revenue and gross profit did the business generate?
2. What was the overall gross profit margin?
3. How did revenue and gross profit change across fiscal years?
4. Which products generated the highest gross profit?
5. Which products recorded the highest gross profit margins?
6. Which products sold in the highest quantities?
7. Which sales territories contributed the most to total gross profit?

## Analysis Performed

The project involved the following analytical activities:

- Data cleaning and transformation using Power Query.
- Data quality checks to identify missing values, errors, and unmatched records.
- Data modelling using a star schema.
- Creation of calculated columns and DAX measures.
- Analysis of revenue, product costs, gross profit, and gross profit margin.
- Comparison of sales and profitability across fiscal years.
- Identification of top-performing products by quantity sold, gross profit, and gross profit margin.
- Evaluation of gross profit contribution across sales territories.
- Development of an interactive Power BI dashboard with KPI cards, charts, and slicers.

## Key Findings

- Total revenue generated was approximately $109.81 million.
- Total product cost was approximately $97.26 million.
- Total gross profit was approximately $12.55 million.
- Overall gross profit margin was 11.43%.
- Revenue and gross profit increased across FY2018, FY2019, and FY2020.
- FY2020 recorded the highest revenue and gross profit.
- The United States generated the highest total gross profit among the sales territories, while Australia recorded the highest gross profit margin.
- Mountain-200 product variants were among the leading products by gross profit.

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following actions are recommended:

- Focus on strategies that sustain revenue growth while maintaining control over product costs.
- Prioritize products that generate strong gross profit and healthy profit margins.
- Review lower-margin products to identify opportunities for cost optimization without compromising product quality.
- Strengthen sales strategies in territories with strong profitability performance.
- Monitor revenue, gross profit, and gross profit margin regularly to support informed business decisions.
